What it does

One pass from rough take to finished track

Everything you would normally chain by hand, in the right order.

Clean

Removes noise, mic pops, harsh S sounds and loud breaths, repairs clipped peaks, evens out levels and dries up room echo.

Tune

Reads the key from your beat and tunes to it: off, natural, hard-tune, or a style learned from a reference song.

Fit to the beat

Detects tempo and key, nudges words onto the beat, and sets the vocal level against the music.

Sound like an artist

Drop in a song you love. VocalForge measures its tuning, tone, space and width and applies a similar treatment to your voice.

Mix live

Paint hooks, verses and ad-libs on the timeline and give each its own reverb, echo, doubles and harmony. Changes are instant.

Master and export

Loudness, true-peak and tonal checks with a pass/fail report, then export 24-bit WAV or FLAC.

Sound like the records you love

Drop in a song. Get that treatment on your voice.

Pick any track by an artist whose sound you want. VocalForge separates the vocal and measures how it was tuned, brightened, spaced, doubled and compressed, then applies a similar treatment to your recording.

  • Learns the autotune style: natural, modern or hard-snapped
  • Matches tone, reverb size, width and how tight the flow sits on the beat
  • Keeps your voice yours: it changes the processing, not who you are
  • Saves each style so you can reuse it on the next song

Not affiliated with or endorsed by any artist. Artist names are used only to describe styles of processing.

Private by design

Your unreleased songs never leave your PC.

VocalForge processes everything locally. No uploads, no per-minute limits, no watermarks. The only internet use is the license check and the optional AI add-on download.

Who it’s for

Made for people who record in real rooms

Bedroom rappers and singers

Recorded on a phone or USB mic and want the take to sound finished next to the beat.

Producers finishing demos

Turn a rough artist take into something you can send back or release.

Anyone whose vocal feels “off”

Timing that drifts, words that sound cut, levels that jump: fixed before you post.

Honest limits: VocalForge is a finishing tool, not a DAW or a VST plugin (yet), and it is Windows-only for now. It can improve a rough recording a lot, but it can’t turn a vocal buried in echo or heavy distortion into a studio take. Re-recording closer to the mic is always the best fix.

Questions

Is VocalForge a plugin or a standalone app?

A standalone Windows app. You drop in your recording and your beat, and it exports finished WAV or FLAC files you can use anywhere, including inside your DAW. It is not a VST plugin yet, and it is not a DAW: think of it as the finishing step before you post.

Does it need an internet connection?

Your audio is processed on your own PC, so your unreleased songs are not uploaded anywhere. The app checks your license online and works offline for up to 14 days after a successful check. The optional AI vocal-separation add-on is a one-time download.

What does the free trial include?

Seven days with everything unlocked. After the trial you can still open and listen to projects, but processing and exporting need a license key.

How many computers can I use one license on?

Two. If you get a new PC, deactivate the old one from the License screen to free the slot.

Do I need a powerful computer or a GPU?

No. Cleaning, tuning, mixing and mastering run on a normal Windows 10 or 11 PC with 8 GB of RAM or more. A recent NVIDIA graphics card only makes the optional AI vocal-separation step much faster; without one it still works, just slower.

What file types can I use?

WAV, FLAC, MP3 and OGG work out of the box. Exports are 24-bit WAV or FLAC. M4A and AAC files work if ffmpeg is installed on your PC.

What if my recording is really bad?

VocalForge can repair a lot (noise, clicks, mic pops, harsh S sounds, clipped peaks, level swings, room echo), but it is not magic. A vocal buried in reverb, heavily distorted, or recorded far from the mic will improve but not become a studio take. Re-recording closer to the mic is always the best fix.

Is this AI?

Mostly classic signal processing that runs locally. An optional AI model is used for one job: separating vocals from a full song, for example to remove the vocal from a beat you downloaded or to study a reference track.

Can it make me sound like a specific artist?

You can drop in any song by an artist whose sound you like. VocalForge measures how that record was tuned, EQ-ed, spaced and mixed and applies a similar treatment to your own voice. It changes the processing, not your voice, so you will still sound like you. VocalForge is not affiliated with any artist.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) only for now.
