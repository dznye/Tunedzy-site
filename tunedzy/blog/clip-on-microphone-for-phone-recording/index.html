How to Use a Clip-On Microphone for Phone Recording

Updated October 2026 · Phone recording

Quick answer

Clip the microphone about 15 to 20 centimetres below the mouth, on the chest or collar, with the cable hidden and no rubbing. Connect it to the phone with the correct adapter, then record a short test and check for rustle and handling noise before you start.

A clip-on microphone is one of the easiest upgrades for phone recording. It keeps the microphone close and steady, which is the most important factor for clear speech, and it frees both hands. It is especially useful for interviews, lectures and voice notes where the speaker moves.

Choose the right connection

Clip-on microphones connect to a phone in different ways, such as a wired plug, a lightning or USB-C connector, or a wireless link. Check that the connector suits your phone before you buy, and use the adapter that came with the microphone. A wireless microphone needs its own charging and pairing, so test it before the session.

Place it well

Clip the microphone about 15 to 20 centimetres below the mouth, on the chest or the collar, facing up. Keep it away from the necklace, the scarf and the jacket zip. Hide the cable under the clothes, with a little slack, so it does not rub or pull.

Stop the rustle

Clothing rustles against a clip-on microphone, and the sound can be loud. Choose a spot where the fabric is still, and use a foam windscreen or a small cover. Ask the speaker to keep their arms relaxed, and avoid resting on the microphone.

Test before recording

Record a short test with the speaker talking at normal volume, then move, turn and lean slightly. Play it back on headphones. Check for rustle, handling noise and any change in level when the speaker turns their head.

Keep the phone and the microphone apart from the table

Put the phone on a soft cloth or a stand, so table vibration does not reach the recording. If the phone is connected by a cable, keep the cable from pulling on the table or the speaker’s clothes.

Limits

A clip-on microphone improves clarity, but it does not stop room echo or background noise. Pair it with a quiet room and a steady setup. For multiple speakers, each person needs their own microphone, or the sound will be uneven.

Frequently asked questions

Where should a clip-on microphone go?

About 15 to 20 centimetres below the mouth, on the chest or collar, facing up.

Why does my clip-on rustle?

Clothing is moving across the microphone. Choose a still spot and add a small windscreen.

Do I need an adapter?

Often yes. Check the connector on your phone before you buy the microphone.

Can one clip-on record two people?

It can, but the sound will be uneven. Give each person their own microphone when you can.
