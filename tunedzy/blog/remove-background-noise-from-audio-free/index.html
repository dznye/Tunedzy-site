How to Remove Background Noise From Audio for Free

Updated October 2026 · Voice recording and noise removal

Quick answer

Drop the file into a free in-browser noise remover that learns the noise from a quiet part of your recording, or use Audacity’s Noise Reduction (12 dB, sensitivity 6, smoothing 3 as a start). Both work best on steady noise such as hiss, hum and fans, and neither can remove other voices.

Way 1: A private in-browser noise remover

Open the voice recorder with noise cancellation and use Clean an existing recording. Drop in your MP3, WAV, FLAC or OGG file. The tool looks for a quiet stretch to learn your noise from, removes it, and gives you a before-and-after player and a WAV download. Because it runs in your browser, the file is never uploaded, which matters for private or unreleased audio.

Way 2: Audacity (free desktop editor)

  1. Select 1–2 seconds of pure background noise (a pause with no speech).
  2. Open Effect → Noise Reduction and click Get Noise Profile.
  3. Select the whole track, open Noise Reduction again, and start with 12 dB reduction, 6 sensitivity and 3 frequency smoothing.
  4. Press Preview, then OK. Undo and lower the dB if the voice sounds thin.

Way 3: Your video editor

Most free video editors have an audio denoise or “reduce noise” effect. They are fast and convenient but give you few controls, so they suit light hiss rather than heavy noise.

What you can and cannot remove

  • Works well: steady hiss, hum, fans, air conditioning and computer noise.
  • Works partly: distant traffic and constant crowd murmur.
  • Does not work: another person talking, music, room echo, clicks and coughs. Those need a re-record or a more advanced repair.

Tips for better results

Leave 1–2 seconds of silence at the start of any recording so noise can be learned, apply noise reduction only once, and work from the original WAV rather than a compressed MP3 whenever you can. If the voice sounds watery, read why noise reduction does that and how to fix it.

Frequently asked questions

How can I remove background noise from audio for free?

Use a free in-browser noise remover, Audacity’s Noise Reduction, or your video editor’s denoise effect. Steady noise responds best.

Is it safe to upload audio to online noise removers?

Many online tools upload your file to a server. A tool that runs entirely in your browser keeps the audio on your own device.

Can noise removal fix a very noisy recording?

It can reduce steady noise a lot, but it cannot rebuild a voice that is buried in noise. A closer, quieter re-record is always better.
