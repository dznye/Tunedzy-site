# How similar audio apps structure their search pages (observed 25 October 2026)

What this is: a read of publicly visible sitemaps and search results for the audio apps closest to Tunedzy, and what we take from them.
It is not a traffic or ranking measurement. Sitemap counts below are only what the fetch returned, and some sitemaps were cut off.

## What the competitors publish

| Site | How the sitemap is organised | What the pages are for |
|---|---|---|
| Cleanvoice (cleanvoice.ai) | One sitemap with many single-task pages: an audio enhancer, a recorder, a breath remover, a trimmer, a volume tool, a speed and pitch tool, a podcast audit, and an API page for Adobe Podcast users. Language versions sit under locale prefixes such as /bg/. | Each page targets one task, with the tool on the page. |
| LALAL.AI (lalal.ai) | A sitemap index with three children: main pages, blog, and an API section. | The split keeps blog and API pages apart from the product pages. |
| Moises (moises.ai) | Around 19 language versions, each with the same set of pages (home, features, an AI studio page, a feature page per tool). Also feature pages (vocal remover, key detector, AI mastering, transcription), audience pages ("made for" a worship team), a "for education" page, a "how to" page and a guitar-practice page. | Localised clones of the core pages, plus one audience page per use. |
| Descript (descript.com) | A sitemap index with two children: landing pages and blog. | Landing pages for product and use, separate from the blog. |

## What we take from it

1. **One page per task.** Cleanvoice's single-task pages are the clearest pattern. Our guides already follow it, and the feature pages added here do too.
2. **Audience pages.** Moises writes for a specific group (church worship teams, students). Our audience routines do the same, and we keep them specific.
3. **Feature pages for brand search.** People search a product name plus a feature. Feature pages (Perfect it, Repair, Studio sound, Multitrack, transcript, Auto-duck, replace audio in a video, vocal remover, key and speed, Live Studio, Tunedzy Go) answer those searches directly.
4. **Roundups with the product included.** "Best X" pages list several tools and say where each fits. They rank for the comparison search, and they are useful only when the descriptions are fair. Ours name each tool's real strength, including where it is better than Tunedzy.
5. **Localisation later.** Moises translates its core pages into about 19 languages. We have not started translating; that is a large job and the next step after the English set is measured.
6. **Keep the sitemaps current.** The competitors' sitemaps carry dates from the last few days. Ours are rebuilt on every publish.

## What we will not copy

- Thin pages with no real content per URL. Cleanvoice and Moises have long sitemaps, but each page has a working tool or a real guide behind it; ours must do the same.
- Pages that claim a feature the product does not have. Every Tunedzy feature named on a page is checked against the app.
- Competitor-benchmark self-promotion. Some sites publish "we beat everyone" pages with no test data. We do not publish those.
